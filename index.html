What happens after you submit your quote request

  1. Confirmation. The page confirms receipt and shows your answers, so you can check them.
  2. Engineer review. Our engineers assess the product, container, closure, output and site against what you sent.
  3. Follow-up, if needed. We ask only for what is missing, usually by WhatsApp or email.
  4. First reply. Once we have enough detail, you receive a recommended configuration, the reasons behind the main choices, an indicative price and an estimated delivery schedule. For a complete line, a station diagram and a layout drawing follow for your approval. A project that needs custom design may call for a technical discussion before pricing.
  5. Formal quotation. When the specification is agreed, a formal quotation follows. Drawings are approved before manufacture, and the finished machine runs your own samples before it ships.

Indicative price and formal quotation. The indicative price shows the level your project sits at. The formal quotation fixes the specification, price and terms. If your container sizes, output target or specification change after the first reply, tell us. We revise the configuration and the figure to match.

We usually reply within one working day. A full line proposal can take longer, and it then states a date.

Questions before you submit

How do I request a packaging machine quote if I only know my product?

Fill in the five required answers and add whatever else you know. The engineers ask for the rest, usually by WhatsApp or email. If a form is too much, use the quick start route and send three things by message.

Do I have to complete all six steps?

No. The form has six steps, but only five answers are required, along with the privacy acknowledgement. Each extra answer makes the first reply more exact, and a short brief still gets a reply.

What is the difference between a filling machine quotation and a packaging line price request?

A filling machine quotation covers one station and depends mostly on the product, the fill volume and the number of heads. A packaging line price request covers a complete line: every station you choose, from bottle infeed to collection, sized for one output rate. Choose the right option in the first field, or pick Not sure and we advise.

Can I get a packaging quote without using the form?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your product, a photo of the container and your target output. The engineers reply with questions or a first recommendation. The form gives the most exact reply because it also covers the site and the commercial terms.

Can I ask for more than one machine?

Yes. Select the main machine in the first field and list the others under Anything else. If the machines work together, a complete line may be the better route.

Is the price in the first reply final?

No. The first reply gives an indicative price with a suggested configuration. A formal quotation follows once the specification is agreed, and your quotation and contract set the terms for your order.

How soon will I hear back?

We usually reply within one working day on China Standard Time (UTC+8). Eligible stock machines can usually be dispatched within 7 days of order confirmation. Custom machines are built in 15 to 20 days, plus shipping to your port.

Can I send physical samples?

Yes, and samples give the most reliable result. Say so in the form, and we reply with where to send the bottle, cap and label. For the finished machine, we run your own samples before shipment and send photos and video.

Can I use this form for spare parts or a machine fault?

No. Both have a route of their own, and the right engineer sees them sooner there. Spare parts need the model, the serial number and a photo of the part. A fault needs a short video.
